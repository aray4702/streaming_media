# 7.9 Simulcast send

Section 7, *Video quality and processing*, of [Problems WebRTC solves](../../02-webrtc-problems-solved.md). Problems addressed:

- Heterogeneous receivers in group calls -> Simulcast and SVC; the SFU forwards a matching layer per receiver

## The issue

In a group call through an SFU every receiver gets the sender's video over its own link: a phone on cellular and a desktop on fibre want very different bitrates, and the SFU cannot re-encode. One stream at one rate either starves the fast receivers or freezes the slow ones. The sender therefore sends the track as several independent layers at different resolutions and rates, each on its own SSRC and named by a RID, and the SFU forwards a layer per receiver. The sender's own uplink still has one estimate: the layers must share it, the expensive top layer must turn off cleanly when the uplink drops and come back without oscillating, and a layer that comes back needs a keyframe.

## Design

Simulcast layer configuration as libwebrtc M157 (C17, issue #1162): - video/config/simulcast.{h,cc}: the default layers of a simulcast stream,

```
   with the bitrate table by resolution (kSimulcastFormatsVP8 for VP8, H.264
   and generic; kSimulcastFormatsVP9 for VP9, AV1 and H.265), bitrates
   interpolated between its rows, the maximum layer count by resolution
   (LimitSimulcastLayerCount, with WebRTC-SimulcastLayerLimitRoundUp's 0.1
   default round-up ratio), the legacy conference-mode screenshare layers,
   and the boost of the top layer's maximum to a stream maximum;
```

- video/config/encoder_stream_factory.{h,cc}: the layers a VideoEncoderConfig

```
   and an input frame size make (CreateEncoderStreams): the layer
   resolutions (1/2 steps from the top, or scale_resolution_down_by, on a
   resolution normalized to the scale factor), the bitrate table's limits
   with the application's per-encoding overrides (OverrideStreamSettings),
   and the single-stream default (CreateDefaultVideoStreams).
```

Field trials are explicit fields of SimulcastTrials, at M157's defaults. Not ported: scale_resolution_down_to (the VideoAdapter path), the VP9 SVC branch of CreateDefaultVideoStreams, scalability modes and NormalizeSimulcastSizeExperiment (WebRTC-NormalizeSimulcastResolution, unset by default). Pure functions: no clock, no state.

Simulcast rate allocation as libwebrtc M157 (C17, issue #1162): - modules/video_coding/utility/simulcast_rate_allocator.{h,cc}: the stream's

```
   target split over its simulcast layers. Each active layer, lowest first,
   gets up to its target bitrate while the stable rate covers its minimum
   (raised by the 1.2 hysteresis, 1.35 for screen content, for a layer that
   was off at the last allocation); a layer that can't be covered and every
   layer above it get nothing (the allocation is then bandwidth limited); the
   rest, up to its maximum, goes to the top layer allocated. The first active
   layer always gets at least its minimum. Each layer's rate is then split
   over its temporal layers (kLayerRateAllocation, 40/20/40 for three) in
   whole kbps. Without simulcast streams the codec's own min/max apply.
```

- modules/video_coding/utility/simulcast_utility.{h,cc}. - api/video/video_bitrate_allocation.{h,cc}: VideoBitrateAllocation. - SetupCodec: VideoCodecInitializer::SetupCodec's VideoCodec for a set of

```
   VideoStreams (modules/video_coding/video_codec_initializer.cc, not in
   compare/webrtc/reference/; written from M157's class): per-stream min,
   target and max in kbps, the codec minimum the lowest stream's (at least
   kEncoderMinBitrateKbps, 10), the codec maximum the sum of the streams'
   maximums, the frame size the largest stream's.
```

Field trials are explicit config, at M157's defaults. Pure computation: no clock.

Send-side controller for a simulcast video track (C17, issue #1162): the library's equivalent of libwebrtc M157's VideoStreamEncoder driving a SimulcastEncoderAdapter, with VideoSendStreamImpl's allocation limits. Per captured frame it decides, for each simulcast layer, whether to encode it, as a keyframe or not, at what rate and resolution; the app runs one encoder per layer (the adapter's one encoder per stream) and hands each encoded frame to the session on that layer's send stream (Session::AddVideoSend per layer, each with its SSRC, RTX SSRC and RID).

What it ports: - the layers: EncoderStreamFactory::CreateEncoderStreams for the input size

```
   (video::CreateEncoderStreams: the bitrate table by resolution, the layer
   count limited by resolution, 1/2 resolution steps) and
   VideoCodecInitializer::SetupCodec (video::SetupCodec), on the first frame
   and whenever the input size changes (ReconfigureEncoder: every layer's next
   frame is a keyframe, the frame dropper is reset, a new bitrate adjuster);
```

- the allocation limits VideoSendStreamImpl::OnEncoderConfigurationChanged

```
   hands the BitrateAllocator (SimulcastAllocationLimitsFor): min the lowest
   layer's minimum, max the sum of the active layers' maximums, and the pad-up
   rate CalculateMaxPadBitrateBps (the lower active layers' targets plus
   min(1.2 x the top layer's minimum, its target), as no ALR probing is set);
```

- VideoStreamEncoder::UpdateBitrateAllocation with SimulcastRateAllocator

```
   (video::SimulcastRateAllocator: layers on lowest first while the target
   covers their minimums with the 1.2 hysteresis, the rest to the top layer),
   then EncoderBitrateAdjuster::AdjustRateAllocation per layer
   (cc::SimulcastEncoderBitrateAdjuster), at the same times as
   rtp::VideoSendController: an allocation update, a reconfiguration, and every
   1 s (kParameterUpdateIntervalMs) for a frame past the congestion-window drop;
```

- SimulcastEncoderAdapter::SetRates: a layer whose allocation is below 1 kbps

```
   is paused (not encoded); a paused layer that gets a rate needs a keyframe,
   and SimulcastEncoderAdapter::Encode then makes every active layer's frame a
   keyframe (its legacy behaviour; is_keyframe_needed);
```

- keyframe requests through EncoderRtcpFeedback: a PLI or FIR on any layer's

```
   SSRC asks for a keyframe on every layer (VideoStreamEncoder::SendKeyFrame()),
   at most one per 300 ms; with `per_layer_keyframes` (the codec's
   x-google-per-layer-pli=1 or WebRTC-Video-PerSsrcKeyframes) only on the layer
   named, with an interval per layer;
```

- the encodings' active state (RtpEncodingParameters::active, issue #1173): an SFU that pauses a layer

```
   (a=simulcast with ~rid) or leaves it out sets it through pc::PeerEndpoint on the session
   (Session::SetVideoSendActive), as SetRemoteDescription's UpdateSimulcastLayerStatusInSender calls
   WebRtcVideoSendStream::SetRtpParameters. A change reconfigures the encoder at once
   (CreateVideoEncoderConfig copies `active` into simulcast_layers; VideoStreamEncoder::ConfigureEncoder runs
   ReconfigureEncoder when the frame size is known): the inactive stream is not encoded (SimulcastEncoderAdapter
   gets no rate for it), SimulcastRateAllocator gives its rate to the active streams, and the allocation limits
   count only the active streams (OnEncoderConfigurationChanged: the maximum, the pad-up rate). The encoder is
   re-initialized only when RequiresEncoderReset says so: a stream turned off needs none, a stream turned back
   on does, and every layer's next frame is then a keyframe (next_frame_types_). With every encoding inactive
   nothing is encoded (VideoSendStreamImpl then stops the stream, which is not ported: the allocation limits
   are left as they were);
```

- VideoStreamEncoder's drops for the whole frame, in its order: the

```
   congestion-window drop (OnFrame), the paused encoder (the frame is held as
   pending_frame_ and may be encoded on resume, MaybeEncodePendingFrame), and
   the frame dropper on the stream's total target, filled with every layer's
   encoded size (RunPostEncode per encoded image).
```

Quality scaling is off for simulcast, as libwebrtc's WebRtcVideoSendStream::CreateVideoEncoderConfig allows it only for one active layer (is_quality_scaling_allowed), and so is the initial frame drop. Not ported (C17 remaining work in docs/design/libwebrtc-parity.md 10.x): CPU adaptation (EncodeUsageResource), per-layer frame-rate limits (FramerateController for a layer whose max_framerate is below the input), temporal layers (the encoders here have one), scale_resolution_down_to. Takes `now`, reads no clock, starts no thread, has no callbacks.

C17 (issue #1162; Owner decision #1160: group calls through a LiveKit SFU). See the [libwebrtc parity inventory](../../design/libwebrtc-parity.md#1024-simulcast-send-c17-1162) for the differences from libwebrtc. `video/config/simulcast.cc` and the simulcast path of `encoder_stream_factory.cc` give the layers (the bitrate table by resolution, the layer count limited by resolution, 1/2 resolution steps), `simulcast_rate_allocator.cc` splits the target (layers on lowest first while the target covers their minimums, with the 1.2 hysteresis for one that was off; the rest to the top layer), `rtp::SimulcastSendController` runs VideoStreamEncoder with a SimulcastEncoderAdapter (one encoder per layer, a paused layer below 1 kbps, a keyframe on every layer when one comes back, PLI on any layer keys every layer), the session sends each layer on its SSRC with its RID (repaired RID on RTX) and SRs, and the endpoint offers a=rid/a=simulcast. A layer the SFU pauses (a=simulcast:recv with ~rid) or leaves out is inactive (`RtpEncodingParameters::active`, #1173): the endpoint puts the state on the session (`Session::SetVideoSendActive`), and the controller reconfigures the encoder as `WebRtcVideoSendStream::SetRtpParameters` -> `VideoStreamEncoder::ReconfigureEncoder` does: the inactive layer is not encoded, the allocator gives its rate to the active ones and the limits count only them; `RequiresEncoderReset` re-initializes the encoder only when a layer comes back, which keys every layer.

## Verification notes

Unit tests port `simulcast_unittest.cc`, the applicable `encoder_stream_factory_unittest.cc` cases and `simulcast_rate_allocator_unittest.cc`; `SimulcastSend.*` checks RID/RRID on the wire, the allocation limits (720p: min 30, max 3400, pad-up 1370 kbps) and the adapter rules; `PcPeerEndpoint.Simulcast*` the offer and the SFU's answer. `sm_compare` runs libwebrtc's VideoSendStream with three SSRCs and RIDs and its SimulcastEncoderAdapter over the shared encoder model: the four `simulcast_*` scenarios have no gap on 10 seeds (and `simulcast_capacity_drop_low_layer` none on 20). Two fixes it needed beyond the port: the pacer's round robin between SSRCs and its per-SSRC keyframe flush (`PrioritizedPacketQueue`), and SRs per layer (without them layers 1-2 had no RTT and resent more). #1173: `SimulcastSend.InactiveLayer*`, `ReactivatedLayer*`, `SessionActiveState*` and `RequiresEncoderReset*` check the pause (no frame or packet on the layer, its rate to the others, the 900 kbps limit) and the resume with a keyframe; `PcRenegotiation.SfuReofferPausesAndResumesASimulcastLayer` an SFU's re-offers pausing and resuming the top layer; `issue_1173_simulcast_pause` runs it against libwebrtc's `ReconfigureVideoEncoder` with the layer inactive in `sm_compare`.
