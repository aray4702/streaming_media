# 6.5 High-pass filter

Section 6, *Audio quality (APM)*, of [Problems WebRTC solves](../../02-webrtc-problems-solved.md). Problems addressed:

- Low-frequency rumble or DC offset -> high-pass filter

## The issue

Many microphones and ADCs add a DC offset, and the environment adds low-frequency energy below the speech band: mains hum at 50/60 Hz, handling noise, wind, desk vibration. Speech carries almost no useful information below about 100 Hz, but this energy dominates level measurements. It confuses VAD and AGC (which react to rumble as if it were speech), wastes encoder bits, and can drive the echo canceller's adaptation. It must be removed before everything else, without colouring the speech band.

## Design

hum, handling noise, wind) before echo cancellation and noise suppression. 4th-order Butterworth (two cascaded biquads) at 80 Hz: flat above ~150 Hz, -24 dB/octave below the cutoff.

Ported from compare/webrtc/reference/modules/audio_processing/ high_pass_filter.* with libwebrtc's coefficient tables: three cascaded biquads per rate (kHighPassFilterCoefficients16kHz/32kHz/48kHz, utility/cascaded_biquad_filter.*), the first a high-pass section and the other two notches with zeros on the unit circle just below 50 Hz (about 35 and 46 Hz at 16 kHz), so mains hum is taken down by ~67 dB. The pass band above ~150 Hz sits about 0.4 dB down (the first section's gain is 0.93 at high frequencies), so speech loses about 0.2 dB, as with libwebrtc's. One filter per channel.

APM applies it in the full band (`apply_in_full_band`, the default), at the capture's full-band rate, first in the capture chain (AudioProcessingImpl::ProcessCaptureStreamLocked). Only 16, 32 and 48 kHz have tables; libwebrtc DCHECKs on another rate and uses the 16 kHz table in release builds, as this port does.

Cascaded biquads, ported from libwebrtc M157's compare/webrtc/reference/modules/audio_processing/utility/cascaded_biquad_filter.* (direct form 1, float state). Shared by AEC3 (#240) and the `apm v2` high-pass filter (#239).

See the [libwebrtc parity inventory](../../design/libwebrtc-parity.md#6-audio-quality) for the differences from libwebrtc. The capture chain's default is `apm v2` (#565): libwebrtc's filter (`audio/hpf.h`) runs, and the v1 Butterworth runs only with `apm_v2 = false` until it is retired.

## Verification notes

No defects found; the frequency response matches 4th-order Butterworth. `apm v2` (#239): `hpf::HighPassFilter` is libwebrtc M157's filter with its coefficient tables; it passes the ports of `cascaded_biquad_filter_unittest.cc` (`CascadedBiquadFilter.*`), its measured response matches the analytic response of libwebrtc's 16 kHz table within 0.05 dB (`Hpf.FrequencyResponse16kHz`), and on `sm_apm_compare`'s case it gives hum attenuation 67.2 dB and speech -0.2 dB, as libwebrtc (`E2E_HighPassFilter.LibwebrtcHpfAsLibwebrtc`). Its pass band is about 0.4 dB down, as libwebrtc's.
