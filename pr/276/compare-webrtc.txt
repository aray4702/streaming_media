  video.residual_loss_pct: Counts packets recovered by RTX but not by FEC (libwebrtc does not expose FEC-recovered packets), so it overstates residual loss when FEC recovers packets
  video.undecodable_frames: VideoReceiveStream frames_dropped
  repair.fec_recovered: Not reported: libwebrtc does not expose it
  net.<kind>_kbps / _loss_pct: Counted at the forward link by SSRC: audio, video media, RTX with a payload, FEC (ULPFEC in RED on the video SSRC), and padding (a packet with no payload after RTP padding is removed, including RTX with only its sequence number)
  cc.goodput_kbps: Excludes padding-only packets but includes RTX packets libwebrtc sends as padding
  sync.av_offset_*: The audio playout point is AudioReceiveStream's Syncable::GetPlayoutRtpTimestamp, the input libwebrtc's A/V sync uses, read after each 10 ms pull (the test bench reads its jitter buffer's playout timestamp right after the pull)
